**What is ExoMapper?**

When scientists look for Planet X (a hidden giant planet far beyond Neptune) or cool brown dwarfsthey have to compare infrared space photos taken months apart. Doing this by looking closely at thousands of background stars is really hard.

Our web app **ExoMapper**, makes this fun and easy. It is an interactive observatory where anyone can:


**Blink Sky Passes:** Flip back and forth between observation Image A and Image B to spot moving space objects.


**Auto-Fill Coordinates:** Click or drag your mouse over any star on the screen to automatically measure its position and size.


**Run Scientific Checks:** Use our simple checklist to verify that a moving dot is a real candidate (_SNR > 5.0_) before saving it.


**Hear the Candidate:** Listen to an interactive audio feature that turns infrared brightness into fun Doppler sound waves _(with a friendly reminder that space is a vacuum!)_.
