Mission Briefing & Astrometric Methodology

  • Official Calibration Targets: Inspect Barnard's Star, Epsilon Indi Ba & Bb, WISE 0855 ($\alpha = 08\text{h}55\text{m}10.831\text{s}$, $\delta = -07\text{d}14\text{m}42.53\text{s}$), RW Aurigae, R Draconis, or the uncataloged Sector X-9 survey patch.
  • Multi-Epoch Image Algebra: Switch between Video Blink across $t_0$ and $t_0 + \Delta t$ ($\Delta t = 0.5\text{ yr}$), Difference Subtraction ($\Delta I = I_t - I_0$) to cancel static background stars, Epoch Co-Addition ($I_{\text{stack}} = \sum_{k=1}^{N} I_k$) to reveal proper-motion trails, or Chrono-RGB.
  • 6-Detector Focal Plane Coadder: Combine any subset of SPHEREx's 6 HgCdTe detector arrays to achieve an empirical signal-to-noise boost of $\text{SNR} \propto \sqrt{N_{\text{arrays}}}$ while tuning across all 102 near-infrared spectral channels ($\lambda \in [0.75, 5.00]\text{ }\mu\text{m}$).
  • Smart Aperture Extraction: Drag an Aperture Circle, Box, or Proper Motion Vector ($\vec{\mu}$) over a moving anomaly. The workstation extracts $(x_0, y_0, r)$ from $(0, 0, 0)$ and computes proper motion $\mu = \sqrt{\mu_\alpha^2 \cos^2\delta + \mu_\delta^2}$ and photometric variability $\Delta m$.
